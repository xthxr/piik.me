Answers, up front

Frequently asked questions.

What can I create with Piik?

Piik brings together trackable short links, custom QR codes, campaign parameters, bio pages, and a real-time analytics dashboard.

Can I use my own short link names?

Yes. Choose clear, memorable slugs for links you want people to recognize and trust.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Piik is open source and designed to make link intelligence accessible to teams at every stage.
